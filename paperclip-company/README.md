# Ordering Intelligence AI Company Paperclip Company

Import this directory as a portable Paperclip company package. This package is a concrete specialization of `generic-ai-native-company`, not a standalone custom org.

It includes the generic AI-native company base plus the Ordering Intelligence specialization overlay:

- 34 Hermes agents
- 5 teams, including the Ordering Intelligence Specialization team
- 42 inherited generic/GStack skills
- 9 inherited workflow templates
- one product project and one initial MVP task assigned to Product Lead Hermes

Builds restaurant ordering intelligence workflows for menus, POS integrations, demand analytics, operations, and customer ordering.

## Guardrails

- Do not change live menu prices, availability, promotions, or POS mappings without approval.
- Separate order data, demand assumptions, and model inference.
- Protect customer, restaurant, payment, and order history data.
- Do not send customer campaigns or operational commands without approval.
